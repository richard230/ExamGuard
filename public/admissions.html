Frequently asked questions

How do I apply? +

Select an available admission option, complete the four-step guided form, review your summary and submit. The school will review your application and contact you.

What is the application fee? +

Any applicable fee is shown with the admission option when the school has configured one. Follow only the official payment instructions provided by the school.

How will I know if my application was received? +

A successful submission immediately displays an application reference number on this page and generates a downloadable PDF acknowledgement slip. Keep it for follow-up with the admissions office.
