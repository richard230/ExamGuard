Pre-purchased Pool Pricing

Cards can be pregenerated and bought into your pool at ₦150 per card. Once purchased, administrators can assign pregenerated cards to students without generating new batch requests.

How assignment works

Codes are assigned and stored in the student record. If a card expires, administrators can either extend the expiration date or issue a fresh code from the available pool.

Expired Card Actions

Expired scratch cards block access to result checking. Use the Renew button to extend validity by 90 days or Re-issue to assign a brand new code.
