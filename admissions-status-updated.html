Frequently asked questions

How do I apply? +

Select an available admission option, complete the four-step guided form, review your summary and submit. The school will review your application and contact you.

How do I check my admission status and download my admission letter? +

Scroll to the "Check application status" section above and enter your Application Reference Number or registered email address. Once your application is approved, an option to download your official Provisional Admission Offer Letter PDF will appear automatically.

What is the application fee? +

Any applicable fee is shown with the admission option when the school has configured one. Follow only the official payment instructions provided by the school.
